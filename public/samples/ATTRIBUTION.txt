Riffizer sample attribution
===========================

The electric guitar, acoustic guitar, nylon guitar, and electric bass
multisamples are redistributed from Nicholaus P. Brosowsky's
tonejs-instruments project:

https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

The source project identifies the samples as CC BY 3.0 and credits the
underlying sources in its sample-source-info.txt (Karoryfer, University of
Iowa Electronic Music Studios, and Freesound contributor quartertone for the
instruments used here). The samples were trimmed and normalized by the
tonejs-instruments project. Riffizer renames and bundles a compact subset for
offline playback.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The five short drum one-shots were generated specifically for Riffizer and are
included under Riffizer's project license.
